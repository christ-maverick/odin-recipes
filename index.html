There are two ongoing recalls related to Twin Sisters Creamery cheeses. The first, issued by Peterson Company of Auburn, Washington, concerns item #28855 Whatcom Blue and item #29608 Farmhouse Cheese products. The cheeses were distributed to retailers and businesses, including caterers, distributors, and restaurants in Colorado, Idaho, Oregon, and Washington, between Aug. 14, 2025, and Oct. 24, 2025.
